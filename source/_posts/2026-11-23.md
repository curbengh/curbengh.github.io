---
title: Power Pages now supports Microsoft-managed SSL certificate
date: 2026-11-23
---

Power Pages now supports [Microsoft-managed](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-pages/admin/add-custom-domain) free auto-renewing SSL certificate. Existing SSL certificate, SSL binding, [native CDN and WAF](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-pages/configure/configure-cdn) have to disabled first before the option is available. Once it's deployed, CDN and WAF can be re-enabled back.
